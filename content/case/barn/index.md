[barn.pgsty.com](https://barn.pgsty.com/) documents **Barn**, a tool for
booting fixed-IP multi-node Linux labs and isolated macOS VMs on Apple Silicon
or Linux. Spanning 156 Markdown files in English and Chinese, it pairs a warm
Paper-preset illustrated hero with a command-line quick-start preview,
installation guides, and release blog posts.

## What it demonstrates {#what-it-demonstrates}

- Matching custom editorial illustration seamlessly with OINK's warm Paper
  palette.
- Combining a product landing page, installation guide, reference docs, and
  companion blog in one bilingual repository.
