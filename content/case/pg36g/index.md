[pg36g.vonng.com](https://pg36g.vonng.com/) (*PG 三十六计*) is a 36-chapter
practical production engineering book on PostgreSQL and Pigsty. Built on OINK's
Book shell with custom 3D cover artwork, it mounts structured chapters,
reproducible lab bundles, and multi-axis indexes (by capability, incident, task,
and role) into a clean bilingual publication.

## What it demonstrates {#what-it-demonstrates}

- Publishing an original multi-volume technical book with custom cover artwork,
  reproducible lab attachments, and role/incident cross-indexes.
- Mounting chapter trees from outside `content/` via Hugo module mounts while
  keeping OINK's book navigation and stable anchors intact.
