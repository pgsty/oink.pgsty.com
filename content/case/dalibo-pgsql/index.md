[dalibo.pgsql.cc](https://dalibo.pgsql.cc/) publishes twelve open-licensed
Dalibo PostgreSQL training handbooks—from DBA fundamentals to replication,
tuning, and Kubernetes—across 329 Markdown pages in Chinese, French, and
English. The Paper-preset landing page pairs course cover artwork with a
structured curriculum catalog.

## What it demonstrates {#what-it-demonstrates}

- Grouping twelve independent course manuals into one cohesive trilingual
  (`zh`, `fr`, `en`) training portal.
- Using OINK's Paper preset and metadata strips to present open-licensed course
  provenance, editions, and direct course entry points.
