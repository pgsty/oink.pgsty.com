[aiempire.vonng.com](https://aiempire.vonng.com/) publishes a bilingual
Chinese and English online reading edition of *Empire of AI* across 68 Markdown
files. Built on OINK's `paper` preset and Book shell, it features a clean book
cover hero, author and translator contributor cards, sequential chapter reading,
and single-click whole-book print output.

## What it demonstrates {#what-it-demonstrates}

- Using OINK's Book shell and Paper preset for narrative non-fiction with warm
  long-form reading typography.
- Pairing a book-cover hero with contributor attribution cards and whole-book
  print support in both Chinese and English.
