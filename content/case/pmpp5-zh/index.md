[codingpracticegroup.github.io/pmpp5-zh](https://codingpracticegroup.github.io/pmpp5-zh/) is a bilingual Chinese-English study edition of *Programming Massively Parallel Processors (5th Edition)* maintained in [`CodingPracticeGroup/pmpp5-zh`](https://github.com/CodingPracticeGroup/pmpp5-zh/pull/1). A Python build pipeline converts Chinese LaTeX chapters and rendered TikZ figures alongside aligned English text across 24 chapters and 3 appendices, enabling OINK's **Duo View** (`B`) with synchronized scroll across 230 shared heading anchors and KaTeX math.

## What it demonstrates {#what-it-demonstrates}

- Using OINK's native Duo View (`params.ui.compare: true`) and shared heading anchors for real-world side-by-side bilingual technical book translation and verification.
- Feeding an automated LaTeX-to-Markdown + TikZ pipeline cleanly into OINK's Book reading shell with KaTeX math rendering.
