[doc.pg.center](https://doc.pg.center/) is the largest multilingual site in the
OINK family: nearly 5,000 Markdown files combining six core PostgreSQL ecosystem
manuals (Patroni, pgBackRest, PgBouncer, pgBadger, HAProxy, and etcd) in eight
languages (`en`, `zh`, `fr`, `ja`, `de`, `ru`, `ar`, and `es`) alongside eleven
PostgreSQL kernel and variant collections in English and Chinese.

## What it demonstrates {#what-it-demonstrates}

- Scaling OINK's build, split BM25 search indexes, and navigation to roughly
  5,000 content pages across eight scripts.
- Exercising right-to-left (RTL) layout and UI translations for Arabic alongside
  CJK and Latin languages in one repository.
- Organizing seventeen distinct upstream documentation collections under a single
  consistent reading shell.
