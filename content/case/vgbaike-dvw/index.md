[vgbaike.com/dvw](https://www.vgbaike.com/dvw/) (*设计虚拟世界*) publishes a full Chinese translation of Richard Bartle's foundational game-design book *Designing Virtual Worlds*, with translation review hosted in [`lloydsun/book-dvw-zh`](https://github.com/lloydsun/book-dvw-zh). Mounted cleanly at `/dvw/` alongside the VG百科 gaming encyclopedia, it spans 285 Markdown files with a cover-artwork landing hero, whole-book print output, and direct links to the English source.

## What it demonstrates {#what-it-demonstrates}

- Mounting a standalone OINK Book edition under a subpath (`/dvw/`) of a larger existing web portal.
- Pairing original cover artwork with immediate read, whole-book print, and edition action buttons in the hero.
- Exercising OINK's Book reading shell on a long-form non-software humanities and game-design monograph.
