[eliaszhao.com](https://eliaszhao.com/) (*赵杨*) is a bilingual personal technical blog covering databases, infrastructure, quantitative research, and AI engineering, maintained in [`elias-zhao/eliaszhao.com`](https://github.com/elias-zhao/eliaszhao.com). Migrated from the Blowfish Hugo theme to OINK `v1.6.0` on the `paper` preset, it pairs a custom mountain-landscape hero and portrait avatar with multi-topic columns, Duo View comparison, and `llms.txt`.

## What it demonstrates {#what-it-demonstrates}

- Migrating an established personal Hugo site from Blowfish to plain-CSS OINK `v1.6.0` with no Sass or Node toolchain.
- Personalizing OINK's Paper landing hero with full-bleed photography and an author portrait while organizing writing across technical columns (`db`, `infra`, `quant`, `ai`).
