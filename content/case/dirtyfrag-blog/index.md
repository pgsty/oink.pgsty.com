[karmine05.github.io/dirtyfrag-blog](https://karmine05.github.io/dirtyfrag-blog/) (*karmine's notes*) is an English security operations, detection engineering, and agentic AI blog maintained in [`karmine05/dirtyfrag-blog`](https://github.com/karmine05/dirtyfrag-blog/pull/11). Migrated from Blowfish to OINK's `slate` preset with all 17 existing URLs preserved and over 10,000 internal links verified with zero breakages, it features crisp editorial headlines, consistent post covers, and KaTeX math.

## What it demonstrates {#what-it-demonstrates}

- Completing a zero-broken-link Blowfish-to-OINK migration while preserving every legacy article URL and converting shortcodes cleanly.
- Using OINK's Slate preset for a focused, authoritative English security engineering publication.
