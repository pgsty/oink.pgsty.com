[vianlex.github.io](https://vianlex.github.io/) is a personal software
engineering wiki maintained in
[`vianlex/vianlex.github.io`](https://github.com/vianlex/vianlex.github.io).
Spanning 150 Markdown documents across backend languages, frameworks,
middleware, and operations, it uses OINK's landing cards to organize deep
documentation subtrees into an approachable personal knowledge portal.

## What it demonstrates {#what-it-demonstrates}

- Organizing a 150-page personal engineering wiki with topic card shelves on
  the homepage feeding straight into deep documentation trees.
- Maintaining a fast, locally searchable personal knowledge base with zero
  server infrastructure.
