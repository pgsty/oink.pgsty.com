[blog.kinaz.me](https://blog.kinaz.me/) (*Kina's Blog*) is a minimalist bilingual personal blog maintained in [`delete-cloud/blog`](https://github.com/delete-cloud/blog) and deployed to Cloudflare Pages. Built from OINK Starter on the `paper` preset with Chinese at `/` and English at `/en/`, it pairs an unhurried editorial hero and still-life coffee artwork with RSS feeds, Giscus comments, and `llms.txt`.

## What it demonstrates {#what-it-demonstrates}

- Using OINK's Paper preset for a quiet, editorial personal blog with generous whitespace and minimal chrome.
- Running a bilingual Chinese-default / English-peer blog with Giscus comments on Cloudflare Pages.
