[vchord.pgsty.com](https://vchord.pgsty.com/) documents **Vchord**
(`vchordrq`, `vchordg`, and `vchord_bm25`), the scalable vector and BM25 search
extensions for PostgreSQL maintained by PGSTY. Across 366 Markdown files in
English and Chinese, it pairs a crisp vector-architecture hero graphic with
installation matrices, benchmark comparisons, release downloads, and blog posts.

## What it demonstrates {#what-it-demonstrates}

- Presenting dense database-engine architecture diagrams and benchmark sections
  directly inside OINK's Paper landing layout.
- Pairing a multi-extension technical manual with release downloads and author
  blog posts in both English and Chinese.
