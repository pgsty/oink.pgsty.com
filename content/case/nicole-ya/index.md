[nicole-ya.github.io](https://nicole-ya.github.io/) is a personal homepage and study journal maintained in [`Nicole-ya/Nicole-ya.github.io`](https://github.com/Nicole-ya/Nicole-ya.github.io). Using OINK's multi-section navigation, serif editorial hero, Giscus comments, and local Chinese search, it brings coursework, project showcases, curated bookmarks, skills, and growth milestones together under one clean address.

## What it demonstrates {#what-it-demonstrates}

- Using OINK as an all-in-one personal learning portfolio spanning six distinct top-level sections (`courses`, `projects`, `notes`, `skills`, `blog`, and `about`).
- Pairing refined Chinese serif display typography with an approachable personal landing layout.
