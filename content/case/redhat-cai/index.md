[rh-aiservices-bu.github.io/cai](https://rh-aiservices-bu.github.io/cai/) is
the public portal for Red Hat's **Customer Adoption and Innovation (CAI)** team
in the AI Services Business Unit. Built from
[`rh-aiservices-bu/cai`](https://github.com/rh-aiservices-bu/cai) on OINK's
`paper` preset with Red Hat brand tokens, the site configures nine language
trees (`en`, `zh`, `fr`, `es`, `de`, `sv`, `it`, `tr`, `ga`). The same team
also publishes dedicated OINK field manuals such as the
[CAI Field Manual for MaaS](https://rh-aiservices-bu.github.io/CAI-Field-Manual-MaaS/)
and the
[OpenShift AI Migration Field Manual](https://rh-aiservices-bu.github.io/CAI-Field-Manual-Migration-2-3/).

## What it demonstrates {#what-it-demonstrates}

- Applying enterprise brand colors and artwork cleanly on top of OINK's Paper
  preset.
- Scaling OINK's multilingual configuration across nine languages in one site.
- Reusing one theme consistently across a team hub and companion technical field
  manuals hosted on GitHub Pages.
