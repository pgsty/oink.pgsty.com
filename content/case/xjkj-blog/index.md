[blog.xjkj.org](https://blog.xjkj.org/) (*小知小和*) is a personal database and
infrastructure engineering blog maintained in
[`wzemc/blog`](https://github.com/wzemc/blog). Built on OINK's `paper` preset,
it skips a marketing hero and opens straight into OINK's three-column reading
shell with the sidebar, card-grid article list, view switcher, and right-rail
category and tag clouds.

## What it demonstrates {#what-it-demonstrates}

- Using OINK's blog shell directly as the entry experience with no marketing
  landing page required.
- Exposing OINK's card/list/table/gallery index switcher alongside category and
  tag taxonomy rails for fast browsing.
