[pgpro.pgsql.cc](https://pgpro.pgsql.cc/) organizes eight Postgres Professional
training courses (134 lessons, 441 Markdown files) across Chinese, English, and
Russian. Its landing hero embeds a crisp curriculum learning-path diagram so
administrators and developers can jump straight into DBA1–DBA3, DEV1–DEV2, or
QPT tracks.

## What it demonstrates {#what-it-demonstrates}

- Turning a multi-track technical curriculum (441 pages in three languages) into
  an immediately navigable online handbook.
- Using diagram-led hero artwork to communicate course prerequisites at a glance.
