[crib.pgsty.com](https://crib.pgsty.com/) is the product website for **Crib**,
a native macOS offline reader bundling 31 PostgreSQL documentation branches
(6.3 through 20devel) and structured wiki pages in English and Chinese. Built as
a compact 36-page Chinese-first site on OINK's `paper` preset, it showcases a
centered hero leading straight into a full-width dark macOS app preview,
downloads, blog posts, and product docs.

## What it demonstrates {#what-it-demonstrates}

- Presenting a native desktop application with a crisp centered hero and dark
  software showcase frame on OINK's Paper preset.
- Running a focused single-language Chinese product site with docs, blog, and
  downloads in 36 pages.
