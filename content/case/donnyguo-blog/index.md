[blog.donnyguo.com](https://blog.donnyguo.com/) (*Donny 的博客*) is a personal technical blog documenting self-hosted infrastructure and open-source tools, maintained in [`Donnywork/blog.donnyguo.com`](https://github.com/Donnywork/blog.donnyguo.com). Built on OINK's `paper` preset, it pairs a warm illustrated portrait hero with dated posts, projects, a blogroll, and `llms.txt`.

## What it demonstrates {#what-it-demonstrates}

- Giving OINK's default Paper landing shell an immediately recognizable personal voice with a single illustrated portrait beside the hero copy.
- Pinning OINK as a clean Hugo Module with zero theme-file copying for effortless upgrades.
