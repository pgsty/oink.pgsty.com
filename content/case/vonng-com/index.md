[vonng.com](https://vonng.com/) is Ruohang Feng's personal engineering blog and
publication archive, spanning 1,344 Markdown files across Chinese and English.
It organizes long-running columns on databases, cloud infrastructure, AI, and
engineering culture—each with its own RSS feed—alongside an illustrated author
hero, book catalog, and project directory.

## What it demonstrates {#what-it-demonstrates}

- Scaling OINK's blog and taxonomy shell to more than a thousand bilingual essays
  organized into dedicated columns with per-column RSS feeds.
- Blending editorial warmth (Paper preset, bespoke hero artwork, author bio)
  with technical writing primitives like code blocks, callouts, and diagrams.
- Using one personal domain to index essays, books, open-source projects, and
  long-form series.
