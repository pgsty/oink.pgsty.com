[polla.dev/ssg-book](https://polla.dev/ssg-book/) publishes *Static Site
Generators in the Age of AI: Building and Maintaining Content with AI Agents* by
Polla Fattah. Built from
[`polla-fattah/ssg-book`](https://github.com/polla-fattah/ssg-book), the 53-page
site pairs OINK's Book reading shell with lecture slides, curated learning
paths, blog posts, and a playground under a subpath deployment in dark mode.

## What it demonstrates {#what-it-demonstrates}

- Publishing an open-source course book with a dark-first visual identity and
  custom cover artwork in the landing hero.
- Combining Book chapters with companion slides, topic guides, and learning
  paths inside one shared navigation shell.
- Deploying cleanly under a non-root URL subpath (`/ssg-book/`).
