[polla.dev/frontend-book](https://polla.dev/frontend-book/) publishes *Modern Front-End Engineering: From Browser Fundamentals to Production Architecture* by Polla Fattah, author of [SSG Book](/case/ssg-book/). Maintained in [`polla-fattah/frontend-book`](https://github.com/polla-fattah/frontend-book), the 63-page site pairs OINK's Book reading shell with lecture slides, curated learning paths, PlantUML diagrams, and an interactive playground under a clean subpath deployment.

## What it demonstrates {#what-it-demonstrates}

- Scaling OINK's Book shell across a multi-book university textbook series hosted under separate subpaths on one domain (`polla.dev`).
- Combining textbook chapters, lecture slides, learning paths, and Giscus discussions inside one unified shell.
