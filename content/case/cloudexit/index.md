[cloudexit.org](https://cloudexit.org/) publishes an open standard, public cost
ledger, dated evidence registry, case archive, and practical playbook for cloud
exit capability. Spanning 690 Markdown files in English and Chinese on OINK's
`paper` preset, it pairs a sharp centered editorial hero with structured
specification, ledger, and essay sections.

## What it demonstrates {#what-it-demonstrates}

- Using OINK to publish a public standard and evidence-backed registry rather
  than a traditional software manual.
- Pairing a restrained `align: center` Paper hero with six specialized top-level
  content sections (Standard, Ledger, Evidence, Cases, Playbook, Essays).
- Maintaining high-density comparative tables and bilingual essays under one
  shared search index.
