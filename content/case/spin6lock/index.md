[spin6lock.github.io](https://spin6lock.github.io/) (*KK的博客*) is a long-running Chinese engineering blog covering server development, AI code review, and software craft, maintained in [`spin6lock/spin6lock.github.io`](https://github.com/spin6lock/spin6lock.github.io). Migrated from Jekyll to Hugo and OINK's `paper` preset across 217 Markdown posts, it skips promotional landing blocks and opens directly into a clean chronological reading feed.

## What it demonstrates {#what-it-demonstrates}

- Migrating a 200+ post legacy Jekyll archive to Hugo and OINK Paper with fast local builds and `llms.txt`.
- Choosing a quiet, text-first list homepage when readers come straight for recent writing.
