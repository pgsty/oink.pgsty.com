[nekonull.me](https://nekonull.me/) (*Nekonull's Garden*) is a personal
engineering blog and digital garden maintained in
[`jerrylususu/jerrylususu.github.io`](https://github.com/jerrylususu/jerrylususu.github.io).
Across 144 Markdown files in Chinese and English, it separates writing into
long-form essays, short notes, TIL snippets, and chronological archives behind a
minimalist centered landing page.

## What it demonstrates {#what-it-demonstrates}

- Using OINK as a personal blog and digital garden with distinct content streams
  (posts, short shares, and TIL).
- Presenting a quiet, typography-first personal homepage that lets readers jump
  straight into recent writing.
- Running multilingual personal publishing with local search and tag archives on
  GitHub Pages.
