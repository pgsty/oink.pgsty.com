[sunfang3.github.io](https://sunfang3.github.io/) (*孙方*) is a bilingual Chinese-and-English academic index maintained in [`sunfang3/sunfang3.github.io`](https://github.com/sunfang3/sunfang3.github.io). Instead of duplicating notes that live in separate Quarto repositories, it uses OINK Starter as a fast, structured portal—pairing a quiet hero with clean directory cards for textbook companions and frontier research papers.

## What it demonstrates {#what-it-demonstrates}

- Using OINK as a lightweight, bilingual personal academic portal that indexes external book and paper repositories cleanly.
- Keeping a user-level `github.io` hub maintainable with hand-written Markdown cards, Giscus comments, and `llms.txt`.
