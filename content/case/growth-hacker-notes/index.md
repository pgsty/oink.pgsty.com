[growth-hacker.limbo101.win](https://growth-hacker.limbo101.win/) turns a 41-episode Chinese video course on growth hacking into a clean, searchable written companion site. Maintained in [`limboinf/growth-hacker-notes`](https://github.com/limboinf/growth-hacker-notes), the pipeline transcribes video audio locally with FunASR, structures the notes into seven chapters plus Q&A, and publishes them via OINK with local Chinese search, Giscus comments, and `llms.txt`.

## What it demonstrates {#what-it-demonstrates}

- Transforming long-form audio/video courses into a navigable, searchable OINK reading site hosted on a custom domain.
- Keeping raw transcripts and polished Hugo site sources organized side by side in one repository.
