[int18.pgsql.cc](https://int18.pgsql.cc/) publishes Egor Rogov's *PostgreSQL 18
Internals* as a trilingual online book in Simplified Chinese, English, and
Russian (120 Markdown files). It demonstrates OINK's Book shell handling three
scripts cleanly, complete with Duo View comparison, contributor cards, and
whole-book print output.

## What it demonstrates {#what-it-demonstrates}

- Maintaining a technical translation across three scripts (Chinese, English,
  and Russian) with synchronized chapter trees.
- Pairing OINK's Book shell with Duo View side-by-side bilingual reading for
  translation review and technical study.
