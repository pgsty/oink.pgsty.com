[fzb.vonng.com](https://fzb.vonng.com/) (*冯振彪 · 沙漠之箭*) is a family-compiled
digital archive preserving the autobiography, essays, poetry, books, and
photographs of spacecraft test and launch engineer Feng Zhenbiao. Across 601
Markdown files with an English reader guide, it combines OINK's Book reading
shell with **Expo** photography albums and an editorial Paper-preset landing
page.

## What it demonstrates {#what-it-demonstrates}

- Using OINK's **Expo** image albums alongside Book chapters for biographical,
  literary, and photographic preservation.
- Demonstrating that OINK's warm Paper typography works just as naturally for
  humanities and personal archives as for engineering manuals.
- Hosting 601 archival pages with local search and durable, static HTML output
  meant to last decades.
