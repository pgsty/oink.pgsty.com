[le0shy.github.io/my-docs](https://le0shy.github.io/my-docs/) is a personal
writing hub maintained in [`Le0shy/my-docs`](https://github.com/Le0shy/my-docs).
Organized into three shelves—blog essays, a database and cloud-warehouse
knowledge base, and reviews of music, films, and books—it demonstrates how OINK
handles both structured technical reference and personal writing in English and
Chinese.

## What it demonstrates {#what-it-demonstrates}

- Unifying a technical knowledge base, blog, and personal review shelves under
  one navigation bar and local search index.
- Keeping a centered personal landing page clean and scannable in both English
  and Chinese.
