[sanbei101.github.io/blog](https://sanbei101.github.io/blog/) is a popular
Chinese engineering blog and AI coding playbook maintained in
[`sanbei101/blog`](https://github.com/sanbei101/blog). Built on OINK's `paper`
preset, it combines chronological engineering essays with a structured
`ai-book` handbook section under a clean subpath deployment.

## What it demonstrates {#what-it-demonstrates}

- Combining an engineering blog with a structured multi-chapter guide inside one
  compact OINK site.
- Using OINK's Paper preset and centered landing hero to frame topic pillars for
  Chinese technical readers.
- Publishing directly to a GitHub Pages repository subpath with local Chinese
  search enabled.
