[v0yagernb.github.io/maoxuan](https://v0yagernb.github.io/maoxuan/) is a five-volume, 229-article archival reading edition maintained in [`V0yagernb/maoxuan`](https://github.com/V0yagernb/maoxuan). Built on OINK's `paper` preset with vendored theme assets and three subsetted local WOFF2 fonts (~2 MB total, zero CDN requests), it pairs a warm xuan-paper ivory palette and crimson accents with book-style dotted leader tables of contents, clickable endnote links, whole-book printing, per-page `.md` siblings, and `llms.txt`.

## What it demonstrates {#what-it-demonstrates}

- Pushing OINK's Paper preset and Book shell into museum-grade classical and historical text publishing with custom serif typography and dotted-leader volume indexes.
- Shipping subsetted CJK web fonts locally inside the repository for completely offline builds and reading.
- Combining faithful textual presentation for human readers with clean Markdown and `llms.txt` outputs for machine analysis.
