[stashix.net](https://stashix.net/) is the product website and documentation
hub for **Stashix**, an open-source self-hosted comic, manga, and ebook library
supporting CBZ, CBR, CB7, PDF, and EPUB collections. Maintained in
[`Aroy-Art/Stashix-Site`](https://github.com/Aroy-Art/Stashix-Site), it adopts
OINK's `ink` visual preset paired with a custom violet brand color, a top
announcement banner, and a full-bleed product screenshot in the hero.

## What it demonstrates {#what-it-demonstrates}

- Using OINK's high-contrast `ink` preset with a custom brand accent to create a
  bold consumer-facing product identity.
- Combining a marketing landing page, release blog, and technical setup docs in
  a compact 19-page repository.
- Pairing a dismissible announcement bar with product UI previews in the landing
  shell.
