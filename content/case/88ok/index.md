[88ok.github.io](https://88ok.github.io/) (*追风笔记*) is a 60-page personal technical site focused on banking system architecture, distributed systems, payment clearing, and data governance, maintained in [`88ok/88ok.github.io`](https://github.com/88ok/88ok.github.io). Upgraded steadily from OINK `v0.7.0` to `v1.4.0` on the `paper` preset, it pairs bespoke geometric SVG hero art with columns, photography logs, projects, Giscus comments, and browser-local PlantUML rendering.

## What it demonstrates {#what-it-demonstrates}

- Adopting OINK's zero-server browser-local PlantUML renderer for enterprise architecture diagrams.
- Combining technical columns, dated essays, photography logs (`plog`), and project pages inside one Paper-styled personal hub.
