[yongjiexue.io](https://www.yongjiexue.io/) is a 335-page bilingual engineering blog covering distributed systems, databases, AI agents, and cloud architecture, maintained in [`yongjiexue88/yongjiexue88.github.io`](https://github.com/yongjiexue88/yongjiexue88.github.io). Built on OINK's `slate` preset, it organizes a large personal writing archive into dedicated top-level columns (`db`, `ai`, `cloud`, books, and system design) behind a custom illustrated banner hero with KaTeX math and Giscus comments.

## What it demonstrates {#what-it-demonstrates}

- Scaling a personal multi-column technical blog past 300 bilingual Markdown pages with distinct section navigation and local search.
- Combining custom full-bleed hero artwork and avatar framing with OINK's Slate reading shell.
