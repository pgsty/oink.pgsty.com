## About {#about}

Ruohang Feng ([@Vonng](https://github.com/Vonng)) builds
[Pigsty](https://pigsty.io/), a PostgreSQL distribution, and OINK, the Hugo
theme that publishes its documentation. Both grew out of the same problem:
running a database well and explaining how to run it well are the same body of
knowledge, and neither survives being written down badly.

OINK exists because thirteen sites in that family had drifted into thirteen
copies of one theme. It is a hard fork of [Docsy](https://www.docsy.dev/) that
keeps Docsy's content model and replaces the shell, the navigation, the search,
the content primitives, and the typography system — with one rule throughout:
a feature that needs a CDN, an npm install, or a public service the reader
never chose does not ship.
