Two pieces published the same day, meant to be read in order. The first says
what OINK is and what it deliberately refuses to do; the second is the
engineering record underneath that claim — the migrations, the safety
boundaries, the tests, and the arguments that did not survive contact with
eleven production sites.

Read the announcement for the shape of the thing, the diary for why it has that
shape.
